notes

      

        Drag the dividers to resize the panes; double-click one to put it back. The layout is
        remembered between visits.
      

      

        The console under the code is a real irb against the same VM. Locals persist between lines, and anything you redefine in the
        editor is live there the moment you re-run.
      

      

        Solving knows nothing about which side of the equals sign anything was on. It finds the
        equation that mentions the unknown and nothing else unknown, then finds a root of
        left − right — Newton first, bisection when Newton wanders off the branch the
        variable declared. Those two live in solver.rb and know nothing about physics
        at all; they are handed a lambda and a range.
      

      

        Nothing was rearranged to solve backwards. a is not sqrt(c² − b²)
        anywhere in the source — the same stored equation is handed to a root finder with a
        different unknown.
      

      

        within: earns its keep even here. Squaring loses the sign, so the residual for
        a has a root at −3 as well as 3, and bisection scanning from the bottom would
        find the negative one first.
