Reflection — the angle out is the angle in

The smallest law there is, and enough to show what the whole thing is for. Inside the equation block i and rl are not numbers — they are nodes in an expression tree, so == does not answer true or false, it builds an equation and hands it back. Nothing is computed until something asks for a value, which is why the same one-line declaration solves forwards and backwards.

A law is a module, not a class, because a law is not a kind of another law. What the chapters build up is Interface — the surface light arrives at — and each chapter includes one more law into it. Here it knows exactly one.
